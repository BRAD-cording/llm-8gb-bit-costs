To the Editor-in-Chief,

On behalf of all authors of the manuscript identified above, I declare that
there is no conflict of interest. Specifically, and in the categories normally
requested by IEEE:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Support.} This work received no specific grant from any funding
  agency in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors. No equipment,
  software licence, or other in-kind support was provided by a party with an
  interest in the outcome.
  \item \textbf{Financial interest.} No author holds any financial interest in
  a company or organisation that could be affected by the content of this
  manuscript.
  \item \textbf{Employment, consulting, and equity.} No author is employed by,
  consults for, or holds stock or stock options in any entity with a commercial
  interest in the subject of this paper.
  \item \textbf{Patents and intellectual property.} No author has filed, holds,
  or is negotiating a patent or other intellectual property right that is
  related to the methods or findings reported here.
  \item \textbf{Personal relationships.} No author has a personal or
  professional relationship with any editor, associate editor, or reviewer of
  this journal that could be perceived to influence the handling of this
  manuscript. No author has collaborated with a potential reviewer in a way
  that would require disclosure.
  \item \textbf{Other.} No author has any other relationship or circumstance
  that a reasonable reader could perceive as a conflict of interest.
\end{enumerate}

The reported measurements were produced independently by the authors on
hardware owned by the authors' institution and by the authors personally. The
third-party quantizers used are open-source software (MIT and Apache-2.0
licences) and were used unmodified, with a vendored copy published alongside
the authors' own code. The manuscript reports a negative result on a
task-level benchmark (108 LogHub questions, three configurations,
statistically indistinguishable) and does not claim a task-level gain; the
per-item ablation that failed to reach a usable signal-to-noise ratio is
likewise reported rather than omitted. No result in the manuscript was
selected to favour a commercial product.

The manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under
consideration by any other journal. All authors have read and approved the
submitted version and agree to its submission to \emph{IEEE Internet of Things
Journal}.
